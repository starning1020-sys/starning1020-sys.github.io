01

{{t.h1}}

《流》是一件以「錢」為主題的互動裝置藝術。錢每天在生活中進出，帶走父母工作的時間，也換來我們的早餐、制服、車票與補習費，卻很少人想過它是什麼。作品以透明管線模擬金錢流動，發光的「光幣」從頭頂的地球垂下，流過食、衣、住、行、育、樂六顆時間泡沫，最後匯到地上的「虛化的人」：一個半透明、沒有臉的人形，可以是任何人，也可以是觀眾自己。錢本身是空的，它的價值，來自每一個相信它的人。

Flow is an interactive installation about money. Money moves through our lives every day — it takes our parents’ working hours and comes back as our breakfast, uniforms, bus fares and cram-school fees — yet few of us ever stop to ask what it is. Transparent tubes simulate money in motion: glowing “light coins” descend from a globe overhead, pass through six “time bubbles” for food, clothing, housing, transport, education and leisure, and finally flow into a “blurred person” on the floor — a translucent, faceless figure who could be anyone, including the viewer. Money itself is empty; its value comes from everyone who believes in it.

{{t.h2}}

我們是 STARNING 團隊，由 Yuyun Tsao（組長）與 Bingyang Chen 組成。Yuyun Tsao 負責企劃與統籌，Bingyang Chen 擅長電路規劃與程式設計，兩人的專長結合，完成六個生活場景與燈光互動的設計。我們因為志趣相投而組隊，希望透過這次競賽，把日常生活中最常見、卻最少被思考的「錢」，變成一件能讓人停下來看見自己的作品。

We are team STARNING: Yuyun Tsao (team lead) and Bingyang Chen. Yuyun led the concept and planning; Bingyang, who specialises in circuits and programming, designed the lighting interaction. Together we built six life scenes and their light effects. We teamed up because we share the same curiosity, and we wanted to turn the most ordinary and least examined thing in daily life — money — into a work that makes people pause and see themselves.
03

{{t.h3}}

  • 高中生每天都在用錢：早餐、補習費、手機月租、飲料。錢一直在手中進出，卻很少有人停下來想它到底是什麼。
  • 父母用工作的時間換錢，我們再用錢換食物、知識和娛樂。錢其實是一種「能量」，把一個人的力氣搬到另一個人身上。
  • 但一張鈔票本身只是紙，一個帳戶數字只是電子訊號。它有價值，只因為所有人都同意它有價值。

一開始我們只想做一座雕像，討論時卻一直繞回同一個問題：錢到底是什麼？它像一種能量，在社會裡不停流動，帶走一個人的時間，轉成另一個人的生活；但追問到底，它只是一張紙、一串數字。這個「既重要又虛無」的矛盾，成為我們創作的起點。
  • High schoolers spend money every day — breakfast, tutoring, phone bills, drinks. It passes through our hands constantly, yet we rarely stop to think about what it is.
  • Parents trade working hours for money; we trade money for food, knowledge and fun. Money is a kind of energy that carries one person’s effort to another.
  • Yet a banknote is only paper and a bank balance only a signal. It has value only because everyone agrees it does.

We first set out to make a sculpture, but our discussions kept circling back to one question: what is money? It flows through society like energy, taking one person’s time and turning it into another’s life — yet at its root it is just paper and numbers. That contradiction, at once essential and empty, became our starting point.

{{t.h6}}

  • 作品中央站著一尊與真人等高的人形，以霧面壓克力層疊或細紗網製成，輪廓模糊、沒有清楚的五官，像一個被時間沖淡的人。
  • 六條生活的金錢線最後都流進他的身體，在胸口匯聚成一團暖金色的光，像心臟一樣跳動。
  • 人形是空心的：走近才發現裡面除了光，什麼都沒有。錢最後流向的只是一個人，而這個人可以是任何人，也可以是觀眾自己。
  • 錢從世界流到人，所以人是這場流動的終點；但錢的價值來自人的相信，所以人也是它的源頭。終點，就是源頭。
  • At the centre stands a life-size figure of layered frosted acrylic or fine mesh — blurred, featureless, like someone washed out by time.
  • All six golden money lines flow into the figure and gather at the chest as a warm glow that pulses like a heartbeat.
  • The figure is hollow: up close, there is nothing inside but light. Money ends up with a person — and that person could be anyone, even the viewer.
  • Money flows from the world to people, so people are the end of the flow; but its value comes from people’s belief, so people are also its source. The end is the source.

07

{{t.h7}}

  • 作品中的錢不使用新台幣圖樣，而是自創一種虛構貨幣「光幣」。
  • 正面是一隻睜開的眼睛：瞳孔是放射狀的星芒，一側有睫毛、一側是波浪線，象徵「被看見、被相信」與錢的流動。
  • 反面沒有面額，只有中央一個空白圓圈，外圍繞著一圈小點，代表錢的中心是空的。
  • 直徑約 4 cm，以夜光 PLA 3D 列印，不需另外接電；觀眾進場時每人領一枚，用於投選互動。
  • Instead of real banknotes, the work uses an invented currency: the “light coin”.
  • The front shows an open eye — a starburst pupil, lashes on one side and a wave on the other — standing for being seen, being believed, and the flow of money.
  • The back has no value printed on it, just an empty circle ringed by dots: the centre of money is empty.
  • About 4 cm across and 3D-printed in glow-in-the-dark PLA, needing no power. Every visitor gets one on entry to vote with.
