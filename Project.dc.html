Cosmos Condensed

Starting from the competition’s “fashion design” theme, I condensed an entire universe into a single garment.

ByYuyun Tsao
CompetitionTottori Manga Competition
ResultAwaiting results
SubmittedAugust 30, 2026
Medium · FormatPencil, fineliner · Individual

創作動機

本來就對藝術有興趣，看到這個比賽後鼓起勇氣挑戰自己，某天下午臨時起意就動筆了。

畫面元素

我把自己對人生的各種思考都畫進這件衣服，視角從微觀延伸到宏觀。天體、城市、機械、數位和情感被放進同一個身體裡，呼應「宇宙凝縮」。

流淚的眼睛 0 與 1 齒輪 城市與摩天輪 漩渦星系 行星環

最困難的地方

時間很趕，整張作品要在一個下午內完成。我把握時間專注創作，在時限內把腦中想表達的內容完整畫上紙面。

學到什麼・下次想改進

學到如何把抽象的概念轉化成具體的畫面。

這次用的是一般 A4 影印紙，紙質薄、不耐反覆修改，墨線和細節的表現也受限。下次想改用品質較好的畫紙，讓作品有更多修改空間與層次。

Why I made it

I’ve always loved art. When I saw this competition, I decided to challenge myself — one afternoon, on impulse, I picked up a pencil and started drawing.

What’s in the picture

I drew my thoughts about life into this one garment, zooming from the microscopic to the cosmic. Celestial bodies, cities, machines, the digital and the emotional all live in a single body — a universe, condensed.

A crying eye 0s and 1s Gears City and Ferris wheel Spiral galaxy Planetary ring

The hardest part

Time. The whole piece had to be finished in a single afternoon. I stayed focused and got everything I wanted to say onto the page before the deadline.

What I learned · Next time

I learned how to turn abstract ideas into concrete images.

I used ordinary A4 copy paper, which is thin, doesn’t hold up to reworking, and limits line quality and detail. Next time I’ll use proper drawing paper so the work has more room for revision and depth.
