The Taste of Water

Take a sip of water and feel the universe gather within it.

ByYuyun Tsao
Year2026

餘暉灑落金黃，清風纏繞髮絲，落肩昂首，天際漫漫，似舞動之燦爛，似光影斑斕。

望，時空交錯，回首，宇宙抽絲凝光，低落純淨之璀璨。蕩漾漣漪，淚水滑落空寂，攥緊靈魂之血色，閉眸，寂。清透滋潤，撫過頸喉，與時空對話，萬物凝光水色翻湧寂寞，沉鬱氣息融入清純，落寞。

水光漾，風清蕩。

空凝之水色，倘入時光的洪流，我在其中漂泊，沉浮於光影之中，虛無沉潛寂寞，清透轉瞬交錯，眸光閃爍，似夢，光影撲朔，長夜撕扯餘暉，穿透時光的縫，撿拾遺落的空。那是一種颯爽滑落寂靜的凝煉；是一種星辰閃耀的輝影；是一種沉鬱中獨有的溫存，如此純粹、乾淨、清澈，拂去落埃的殘破，輕灑時光的空流。

人生長路漫漫，昂首，純淨之色沾染了世俗的色彩，冰涼液體流注，寒意沁入肌膚，落，舌尖纏繞滋潤，沉著而內斂，注入生命之感動。

潔，那無處安頓的思緒，淨混沌之心靈，讓水順著思緒，沖淡一切苦悶。

一成不變的世界，早已填滿了黑暗，夜色幽微，靜寂。

流淌的清澈，映那眸，乾淨，毫無雜質的透亮，凝晨光、雨露，化為一泉水，劃破黑暗與孤寂之色，何謂空鳴之水？如宇宙軌跡悄然佇足的虛無；如凝望中悄然滑落的淚，消匿於山水間，吸吐清澈之空濛，那是自然的顫抖。水，清透無私；光，穿透璀璨，悄無聲息。

舉杯。

啜飲一口，寧靜沁水淌入頸喉，沖淡世俗沉默，撫去傷疤之血色，清澈滑落眸中，靜，夏夜的蟬鳴；寂，冬日的初雪，輕盈、美好，如水珠滾落的彩影，從此，人生路漫漫，願澈水洗淨黑暗，沉澱，透明，水之味。

Translated from the original Chinese.

Golden afterglow spills down; a clear breeze winds through my hair. Shoulders loose, chin raised, the sky stretches on and on — like a dance of brilliance, like a mosaic of light and shadow.

I gaze: time and space cross. I look back: the universe unspools its threads and gathers light, letting fall a pure radiance. Ripples spread; tears slip into the empty silence; I clench the blood-red of my soul, close my eyes — stillness. Clear and cool, it brushes my throat and speaks with time and space. All things gather light; the colour of water churns with loneliness; a heavy melancholy dissolves into purity — and a quiet sorrow.

Water shimmers; the wind drifts clear.

The hollow, gathered colour of water slips into the torrent of time. I drift within it, rising and sinking through light and shadow; emptiness settles into loneliness, clarity flickers and crosses in an instant, eyes glimmer as if in a dream, light and shadow blur. The long night tears at the afterglow; I slip through the cracks of time to gather the emptiness left behind. It is a crisp distillation sliding into silence; it is the glow of shining stars; it is a tenderness found only in melancholy — so pure, so clean, so clear, brushing away the broken dust and scattering lightly over the empty stream of time.

Life’s road is long. I lift my head; the pure colour has been stained by the colours of the world. Cold liquid pours in, a chill seeps into my skin; it falls, winding around the tip of my tongue, calm and restrained, filling me with the feeling of being alive.

Cleanse the thoughts that have nowhere to rest; clear the clouded heart; let the water follow my thoughts and wash every sorrow thin.

This unchanging world has long been filled with darkness; the night is faint and still.

Flowing clarity mirrors those eyes — clean, flawless, translucent — gathering morning light and dew into a spring that cuts through the colours of darkness and solitude. What is the water of empty echoes? Like the void where the universe’s orbit quietly pauses; like a tear slipping silently from a gaze, vanishing among mountains and rivers, breathing in and out a misty clarity — the trembling of nature. Water, clear and selfless; light, piercing and brilliant, without a sound.

I raise my glass.

One sip: still, cool water flows down my throat, thinning the world’s silence, wiping away the blood-red of old scars; clarity slides into my eyes. Stillness — cicadas on a summer night; silence — the first snow of winter. Light and lovely, like the rainbow shimmer of a rolling drop. From here on, life’s road is long — may clear water wash the darkness away, settling, transparent: the taste of water.
